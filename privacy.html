Privacy Policy

Airlock Scratchpad collects nothing. This page explains what that means in practice, and how it is enforced rather than merely promised.

What is collected

Nothing. Not analytics, not crash reports, not usage statistics, not an email address, not a device identifier, not an IP address.

There is no account to create and no sign-in. The developer has no way to know that you have installed the app, let alone what you keep in it.

Why that claim is verifiable

Privacy policies usually ask you to take a promise on trust. This one rests on something structural instead: the app contains no networking code at all.

  • It makes no HTTP requests, opens no sockets, and links no analytics or advertising SDK.
  • It runs inside Apple's App Sandbox, and its entitlements do not include network access of any kind — neither outgoing nor incoming.
  • macOS enforces that at the operating-system level. Even a bug could not send your data anywhere, because the sandbox would refuse the connection.

The app's complete set of permissions is: run sandboxed, read a file you explicitly choose, and print. That is all of them.

Where your data lives

Everything you store is kept in a single encrypted file inside the app's own sandbox container on your Mac.

  • Encrypted with AES-256-GCM, using Apple's CryptoKit.
  • The encryption key is wrapped by a key held in your Mac's Secure Enclave, which cannot be exported from the hardware.
  • Contents are decrypted into memory only while the vault is unlocked, and dropped when it locks.
  • No copy is written anywhere else — no temporary files, no logs, no caches.

The developer cannot read your vault. Neither can Apple. There is no recovery mechanism, because a recovery mechanism would be a way in.

The clipboard

When you copy a secret, it goes to your clipboard so you can paste it — that is the point of the app. Two things reduce the exposure:

  • Copied secrets are marked as concealed, which well-behaved clipboard-history tools honour by not recording them.
  • The clipboard is cleared after a delay you control in Settings, and only if it still holds what the app put there.

Once a value is on your clipboard, what other software on your Mac does with it is outside this app's control.

Files you import

Importing a .env or Markdown file reads that file and copies the parts you confirm into your vault. The original is never modified, moved or deleted. The app has read-only access to files you pick, and write access is used for exactly one thing: saving a PDF when you choose "Save as PDF" from the print dialog.

Deleting your data

Delete individual items in the app, or empty the Recycle Bin to destroy them immediately. To remove everything, delete the app and its container:

~/Library/Containers/com.rabcon.AirlockScratchpad

Nothing survives elsewhere, because nothing was ever anywhere else.

Children

The app is a developer tool and is not directed at children. Since it collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new effective date. Any change that introduced data collection would also require a new App Store submission and a change to the app's declared permissions, both of which are visible to you before you update.
